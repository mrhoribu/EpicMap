EpicMap 1.2.5 update - 2026-11-25

Copy the included EpicMap folder over your existing Lich5/EpicMap folder.
Keep existing maps, night, world and gs4map-data.js files; do not remove them.
The update replaces the application files and adds the illustrated background.
The Lich companion script and room database are unchanged.

Reload the open map with Ctrl+F5 after the copy completes.
Layers > World background selects Illustrated, Minimal vector or Original.
Your existing browser preferences remain; the new default is Illustrated.

Following keeps your chosen zoom when moving between maps.
Dragging leaves Follow enabled and resumes on the next character movement.
Use F or the Follow button to intentionally pause it.

The illustrated background is decorative and AI-assisted, based on the
credited Simutronics artwork. It is not new official geography or route data.
